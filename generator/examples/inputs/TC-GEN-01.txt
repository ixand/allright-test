TC-GEN-01: Add a product to the cart from its details page

Preconditions:
- The user is logged out and the cart is empty.

Steps:
1. Log in as standard_user with the password secret_sauce.
2. On the Products page, open the details page of "Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt".
3. Click "Add to cart".
4. Open the cart.

Expected result:
- After step 3 the button reads "Remove" and the cart badge shows "1".
- The cart holds exactly one item, "Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt", priced "$15.99".
